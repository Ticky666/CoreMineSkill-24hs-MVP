## What It Is

**CoreMineSkill-24hs-MVP** is a coaching AI skill by Dilin (Mine Vein). It guides you step by step, in a direct and confident way, from "wanting to start a business" to "making your first sale within 24 hours" — first helping you decide what to do and whether it's worth doing, then pushing you to validate it in action.

It covers demand discovery, benchmark analysis, risk assessment, pricing and seed users, helping you avoid betting it all upfront. Built on McKinsey-style structured thinking and Jobs-style user insight, every step turns into concrete action, no empty talk. Designed for solopreneurs, creators, and new-business leads.
